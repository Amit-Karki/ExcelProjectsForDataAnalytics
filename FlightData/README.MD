# Introduction:

This data is extracted from Kaggle  which is about a airline company where customer of different type i.e Frequent and rare customer travelling in different classes(Eco Business and Eco plus) and flight type(Business,Personal)  have reported satisfied or dissatisfied based upon the different features like inflight service, seat comfort etc which is from range 0 to 5. So our project focuses on getting valuable insights from these data also building a attractive dashboard which gives the related employee in the airline company to view total customer count, Satisfaction %, Average age of the customers etc based on the criteria selected by the user.

# Flight Service Dashboard
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d856975a-d0cf-486c-b063-47b5478e0f70

This dashboard allows the end user to basically select the Customer type(Rare, Frequent, All),  Gender(M,F,All), flight type(Business,Personal,All) and Classes(Eco, Business, Eco plus and All) and on the basis of these inputs view the total customer, Average Customer age, Customer Satisfaction and Disatisfaction Percent, bar chart showing the coorelation between the satisfaction and different flight services like inflight Service, Seat Comfort etc in descending order.

 # Excel tools/skills used:
. Formulas and Functions such as AVERAGEIFS if for calculating Average Customer age, SUMFIS for calculating the total customer etc.
. Charts and graphs: Used the bar chart for visualizing the different services in the airplane which affects the customer satisfaction using the coorelation coefficent.
. Sheet Protection: Protected the sheet and allowed to select the data used for input validation only.
. Icons and sheet view: Used icons for showing different data like Gender, Flight type etc  visually also used sheet view feature to hide the gridlines, formula bar and heading.

Key insight generated:
. Usually the customers who visit frequently are more satisfied compared to the customers who visit rarely.
. Person who travels in Business class tends to be more satisfied than the Eco and Eco plus class which may be the use of eco friendly material or services that the customers don't find highly satisfied.
. Usually Male customer tends to be more satisfied in comparison to Female customers.

# Conclusion
This dashboard may be used by the related stakeholder of the airline company to analyze on what basis of customer type, gender, flight type, flight class etc are the customers more satisified and according to it making the decision to improve the customer satisfaction and total customer count. Also a correlation chart is shown which shows due to what particular features of the airline the customer is more or less satisfied.
